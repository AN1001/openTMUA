Q. Algebra Original · OpenTMUA

x and y are positive integers such that x² − y² = 45. How many possible values of x are there?

x² − y² = (x−y)(x+y) = 45. Factor pairs of 45: (1,45), (3,15), (5,9) — each gives an integer (x,y). That's 3 values of x.
